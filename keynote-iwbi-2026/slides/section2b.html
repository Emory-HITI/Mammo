Era II · 2026

2026: two prospective studies show AI screening works.

MASAI · Sweden · RCT (~105,000)
Interval cancers non-inferior (ratio 0.88)

Sensitivity 80.5% vs 73.8% at matched specificity · ~44% less reading workload.

Gommers/Lång et al., Lancet 2026;407:505–514
PRAIM · Germany · real-world (463,000)
+17.6% cancers detected

Recall non-inferior · 6.7 vs 5.7 CDR per 1,000 · 12 sites, 119 radiologists.

Eisemann et al., Nature Medicine 2025

A third is underway: PRISM — the first large randomized trial of screening AI in the United States (Transpara; $16M PCORI; 7 sites; announced Sept 2025, recruiting). Results are years away, but it tests the US single-reader workflow directly.

Era II · 2026

2026: the averages look strong — so where do these models still fail?

Overall AUC 0.91 — but performance depends on the imaging feature. Masses, asymmetries, calcifications are called negative in 60–81% of exams; architectural distortions behave erratically — even cancers split bimodally, and many benign distortions score high.

Era II · 2026 → Era III

We are at the brink of Era III.

Detection is proven. But the same 2026 image — and the tissue that follows it — already tells us more than "is there cancer today?"

From one screening mammogram and its biopsy: a future-risk estimate, a cardiovascular signal, and a molecular recurrence score. The shift from finding the lesion to characterizing the patient — and much of it is available now.

Era III · 2026

Image-based risk: research model → cleared product.

Mirai (Yala et al.) reads 5-year risk from the mammogram itself — validated across 3 countries. Clairity took it to FDA clearance (2025) and the 2026 NCCN guidance: risk from a routine mammogram alone.

Era III · 2026
AsymMirai: much of the signal is local bilateral asymmetry.

AsymMirai (Donnelly et al., Radiology 2024 — Duke + Emory, EMBED 210,067 mammograms) reverse-engineered Mirai's signal: the left-vs-right tissue difference. A transparent model on that alone nearly matched the black box — 1-yr AUC 0.79 vs 0.84; 3-yr AUC 0.92 in stable tissue. Performance you can explain is performance you can audit.

Era III · 2026

The model reports BAC as a continuous burden (mm²).

A transformer segments breast arterial calcification on the routine mammogram and quantifies its area — turning an incidental finding into a graded, quantitative risk marker.

It scales across severity: mild ≈ 5.6 mm², moderate ≈ 18 mm², severe > 100 mm².

Dapamede et al., Eur Heart J 2026;47(18):2206–2220 (Emory + Mayo) · segmentation: Guo et al. (SCU-Net), Med Phys 2021 — your group
Era III · 2026

Dose-dependent, and incremental to PREVENT.

123,762 women, no known CVD (Emory + Mayo) — BAC area shows a continuous, dose-dependent association with MACE and all-cause mortality. PREVENT-adjusted, vs zero BAC:

mild HR 1.32
moderate HR 1.75
severe HR 3.29 int · 2.80 ext

Each +1 mm² adds 2–3% MACE risk (p < .001) — and it is incremental to the AHA PREVENT score.

Dapamede et al., Eur Heart J 2026;47(18):2206–2220 · Kaplan–Meier, any MACE, combined cohort

Era III · 2026 → 2035

And we are on the verge of multimodal, multi-omics.

Everything so far is still one model, one modality — the image, or the calcium, or the tissue, read separately.

The frontier is to combine them — imaging + pathology + genomics + the clinical record — into a single model of the patient. To see where that goes, we have to leave 2026.
