Lesion→Image→Patient

Across the hospital, in the pathology lab.

So far we have stayed in the reading room. The same arc — hand-built tools, then deep learning, then foundation models — has played out in pathology, and it has one capability our images do not: inferring molecular and genomic features directly from the stained slide.

This is also where the third model from the opening lives — the recurrence score read from a glass slide.

Era III · morphology → molecular (the third model)

Estimating the Oncotype DX recurrence score from the H&E slide.

0.89 vs 0.73
flags high-risk (RS>25) vs a leading nomogram
0.75 vs 0.49
predicts recurrence in low-RS patients — beating the score itself

Oncotype DX costs ~$4,000 and takes 1–2 weeks. Orpheus (6,172 cases, 3 institutions) infers the 21-gene Recurrence Score directly from the H&E. This is what imaging cannot do: morphology → molecular identity.

Boehm et al. ("Orpheus"), Nature Communications 2025 · concept: Kather et al., Nature Cancer 2020
Era III · morphology is molecular

Inferring molecular features from the H&E, including invasive lobular carcinoma.

Invasive lobular carcinoma hides in dense tissue and grows single-file — the hardest cancer to catch on mammography. It is defined molecularly by loss of E-cadherin (the CDH1 gene). A model predicts CDH1 status from the H&E alone. The morphology was always a readout of the biology underneath; it just wasn't visible by eye.

CDH1 from H&E · AUC ~0.94
74% of AI-flagged cases had hidden CDH1 inactivation the assay missed
Cancer Research 2024 (Mount Sinai) · PMID 39106449 — a dedicated model, not Virchow
Era III · pathology's reader study

CAMELYON16: AI compared with pathologists on lymph-node metastasis.

0.994
best algorithm · AUC
0.810
pathologist panel · AUC (under a 2-hour limit)

Lymph-node metastasis on H&E, 11 pathologists under realistic time pressure. Under the clock, the best AI exceeded the panel; top algorithms matched experts given unlimited time. The point is fatigue and time, not replacement.

Ehteshami Bejnordi et al., JAMA 2017 · DOI 10.1001/jama.2017.14585
Era III · a call that now picks a drug

HER2-low: AI improves agreement on the 0-versus-1+ call.

Since trastuzumab deruxtecan (DESTINY-Breast04), HER2-low is treatable. That makes the HER2 0-vs-1+ distinction decisive — a call the assay wasn't designed for, on which pathologists disagree.

pathologist agreement 72.4%
automated AI 92.1%
0-vs-1+ reader agreement 70% → 87%
Krishnamurthy et al., JCO Precision Oncology 2024 · DOI 10.1200/PO.24.00353
Era III · combining the two specialties

Analyzing radiology and pathology together.

Radiology and pathology have been handled as separate departments, with separate images and separate reports. AI now makes it practical to analyze them together for the same patient.
