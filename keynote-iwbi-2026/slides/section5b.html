The mammogram as a whole-body biosensor.

The same image she came in for holds more than cancer. Breast arterial calcification (BAC) — discarded as an incidental finding for 40 years — is a sex-specific cardiovascular signal that goes unread on millions of mammograms.

BAC → ASCVD HR 1.51, independent of usual risk factors (Iribarren 2022)
Cardiovascular & opportunistic

The model reports BAC as a continuous burden (mm²).

A transformer segments breast arterial calcification on the routine mammogram and quantifies its area — turning an incidental finding into a graded, quantitative risk marker.

It scales across severity: mild ≈ 5.6 mm², moderate ≈ 18 mm², severe > 100 mm².

Dapamede et al., Eur Heart J 2026;47(18):2206–2220 (Emory + Mayo) · segmentation: Guo et al. (SCU-Net), Med Phys 2021 — your group
Cardiovascular & opportunistic

Dose-dependent, and incremental to PREVENT.

123,762 women, no known CVD (Emory + Mayo) — BAC area shows a continuous, dose-dependent association with MACE and all-cause mortality. PREVENT-adjusted, vs zero BAC:

mild HR 1.32
moderate HR 1.75
severe HR 3.29 int · 2.80 ext

Each +1 mm² adds 2–3% MACE risk (p < .001) — and it is incremental to the AHA PREVENT score.

Dapamede et al., Eur Heart J 2026;47(18):2206–2220 · Kaplan–Meier, any MACE, combined cohort
Cardiovascular & opportunistic

Beyond the calcium: the whole image predicts cardiovascular risk.

Barraclough et al. (Heart 2026) — DeepSurv on routine mammograms predicts CVD risk in ~49,000 women at C-index ~0.72, comparable to clinical risk equations, without isolating BAC. The tissue itself carries the signal.

Caveats: BAC is not coronary calcium (modest correlation) — an independent marker, not a substitute for CT calcium scoring. And opportunistic predictions need their own prospective validation and a care pathway: a score is useless if no one owns the abnormal result.

Barraclough et al., Heart 2026 (~49,000 women) · BAC vs coronary calcium: Saccenti 2024 (AUC ~0.64)
Cardiovascular & opportunistic

Every image is a richer biosensor than its indication.

Retina → age, sex (AUC 0.97), cardiac events 0.70 (Poplin 2018)
ECG → asymptomatic low EF AUC 0.93 (Attia 2019)
CT → bone density, aortic calcium (Pickhardt 2020)

We can offer tens of millions of women a cardiovascular screen from an image they already came for — at no added cost and no added radiation.

But every one of these models still has to clear the same bar — validation, monitoring, and trust. That is where we go next.
