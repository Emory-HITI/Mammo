The inflection point

2012: deep learning wins ImageNet.

  • AlexNet (Krizhevsky · Sutskever · Hinton) cut ImageNet top-5 error to 15.3% — far ahead of the ~26% runner-up.
  • Trained on two GPUs in a bedroom, then tuned for a year — the spark of the deep-learning boom in vision.
  • The shift from hand-engineered features to learned ones — a decade before Mirai, TEMPO, and modern breast AI.
