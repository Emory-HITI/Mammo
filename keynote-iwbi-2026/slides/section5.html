Era III · multimodal fusion

Fusing the radiology modalities: MG + US + MRI.

Each answers a different question — mammography for calcifications and architecture, ultrasound for the dense breast, MRI for extent. Fusing them improves lesion characterization.

A two-stage MG + US model with modality-specific attention reached AUC ~88% — 13–16 points over either modality alone. Three-tier frameworks now fuse MG + US + MRI + clinical.

Pan/Xu/Fang, J Appl Clin Med Phys 2026 (663 patients). Your group (RSNA 2026): do MG + MRI complement pathology-derived features for pCR? Emerging direction, not trial-level evidence.

Era III · multimodal fusion

BINDS: staged US/MG → MRI, taught to see like pathology.

A two-stage system mirroring real escalation: US/MG first, then MRI for the comprehensive read.

Radiology–pathology alignment: a pathology encoder (trained on whole-slide images) guides the imaging encoders, so the imaging model learns pathology-relevant features.

27,048 participants · 8 centers + 7 public sets
