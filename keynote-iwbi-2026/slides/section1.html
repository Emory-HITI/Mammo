CAD · why it failed

Three reasons it didn't work.

01It flagged what we already catch.Hand-tuned rules aimed at the cancers radiologists already find. So it added marks, not new information.
02Readers learned to ignore it.CAD marked something on ~70% of normal studies. After enough false alarms, you stop trusting the marks.
03It made good readers worse.As an imperfect second reader, it pulled attention toward its marks and lowered sensitivity (OR 0.53).
